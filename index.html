About Lifora

Why Lifora exists

The problem

In an emergency, the first minutes are often spent re-establishing information that already exists somewhere — who the patient is, what they're allergic to, who to call. Lifora keeps that information ready and secure so it can be retrieved, not re-collected.

The approach

Every patient holds a Digital Health ID and a private Medical Vault. Every hospital screen is scoped by role. Every AI suggestion — a triage priority, a document summary — is labelled as decision support, with a qualified professional always able to confirm, modify or override it.

The principle

Minimum necessary exposure. Emergency staff see an Emergency Snapshot, not a full chart, unless further access is authorized and logged. Consent controls sit with the patient wherever the situation allows it.

How it works

A single patient journey, end to end

  1. 01

    Arrival

    An ambulance sends vitals and ETA ahead of arrival, or a patient walks in and shares their QR Health ID.

  2. 02

    Identification

    Secure verification retrieves the patient's Digital Health ID — or opens an Unknown Patient profile with a temporary ID.

  3. 03

    Emergency Snapshot

    Blood group, allergies, medications and major conditions appear immediately for authorized staff.

  4. 04

    AI-assisted triage

    Symptoms and vitals produce a suggested RED/ORANGE/YELLOW/GREEN priority. A clinician confirms, modifies or overrides it.

  5. 05

    Live queue & treatment

    The patient enters the dynamic queue; bed and ward are assigned; escalation alerts fire if waiting exceeds a threshold.

  6. 06

    Family notification

    Configured emergency contacts are notified with hospital, location and status — only what consent and policy allow.

  7. 07

    Ongoing record

    Status moves through Waiting → Treatment → Admitted/Discharged, with every access securely logged.
